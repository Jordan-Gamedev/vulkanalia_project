Notes:

Depth prepass only used on important objects (for SSS) and likely occluders/large objects

Shadow scheme:

    Screen Space Shadows for important geometry
    High Resolution shadow maps for close-to-medium distance geometry
    Shadow volumes for medium-to-far geometry as well as large-volumed geometry

Texture-based AO for the smooth cel-shaded rendering

There will be one realtime reflection probe following the player for great close-medium distance reflections,
while planar reflections will handle all the flat surface reflections

VXGI scheme:

       Use Sparse Voxel Octree Global Illumination (SVOGI)

       Toroidal Addressing: Use modular arithmetic (wrap-around addressing) in your shader lookups for the voxel grid center.
       This allows the physical memory allocation of the 3D texture to stay fixed while logically shifting around the player
       
       Partial Re-voxelization: Only re-voxelize the newly exposed slices/columns of the grid that enter the player's vicinity as they move,
       leaving the overlapping interior intact.

       Sparse Structures: Store your voxel representation in a Sparse Voxel Octree (SVO) or pointer-based lists.
       Empty space consumes zero or negligible leaf allocations,
       dropping memory overhead from gigabytes down to manageable megabytes depending on scene complexity

       Use clipmap cascades for better detail close to the camera

       Use wider voxel cones for smoother, less blocky diffuse lighting
