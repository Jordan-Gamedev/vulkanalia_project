Premise:

You are a little creature alone in the darkness of your little hole you call home.

The fireplace is running out of fuel.

There is this mysterious source of light outside.

You can harvest the light using mysterious crystal prisms found in the pond outside.

Collect the prisms, gather the light spectrum, and bring the prisms home to hang up on the walls
to make your home more pretty and comfortable to live.

However, there is danger lurking in the darkness. Don't stay in the light too long or creature will start to notice

The creatures like shiny objects, so keep your prisms safe.
