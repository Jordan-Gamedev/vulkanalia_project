Title:

-> Schizophrenia: The Game


Art Style:

-> 1) Clean (Cel Shading, Mostly Vertex Colors, Some Smooth Cartoony Textures)

2) Sharp (Simple Lighting, Pixel Art)

3) Realist (Complex Lighting, detailed textures)


Genre:

-> 1) Horror

2) Cozy

3) Casual

4) Competitive


Premise:

You are a detective with a flashlight, a wooden stake melee, and a gun that can chamber both regular and silver bullets

Mission: Take out the vampires taking over the town with minimal civilian casualties

To find out who the vampires are, look at puddle and glass reflections, check if civilians avoid the light,
    hold the bible out towards them, look out for vampires eating other people,
    and shoot them to see if they bleed from the regular bullets
    (since vampires are immune to regular bullets they also wouldn't be scared of being shot)

Vampires can also target you, not just the civilians, so be wary in crowded areas

Your flashlight and silver bullets are highly valueable tools that you need to conserve
